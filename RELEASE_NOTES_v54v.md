# My Life Planner v54v — Today Timed Appointment Priority

**Base:** user-confirmed v54u.

## One functional change
On Home → Today — Time Sensitive, overdue items remain first. Among items due today, appointments with a saved time are shown before untimed items and display their saved time. Timed appointments are ordered by time.

No completion, recurring-task, undo, Lists menu, Timeline, or data-storage behaviour is changed.
