# My Life Planner — Strategic Review Register
**Current baseline:** v54ba
**Reconciled:** 8 September 2026

## Current position
The planner is in active natural-use evaluation rather than feature-expansion mode. Priorities are stability, usefulness and regression avoidance.

## Keep
- Home as an action-oriented dashboard.
- Daily Companion and routines in their current placement.
- Planner Health in its current form.
- Today’s Progress, with the option to hide it.
- Distinct appointment presentation without a completion checkbox.
- Consolidated Pending, Timeline, automatic recovery and manual backup/export.

## Continue to observe
- Home information density/duplication.
- Full Today — Time Sensitive category ordering.

## On hold
- Project Templates / Recurring Projects: redesign from first principles if resumed.

## Product horizon
- Native/distributed iPhone and Android app is a future My Life Planner direction.
- Photography Hub remains separate.
- Accounting App is a possible separate future project.
