# v54s — Undo Ordinary To-do Completion

One functional change only.

- Completed ordinary to-dos and to-do steps now show **Mark incomplete** in Lists.
- Reopening one clears its completion timestamp.
- The matching completion activity record is removed so Evening Reflection and Hidden Statistics do not continue counting an accidental completion.
- Recurring-task undo is deliberately excluded.
- Project-step undo from v54r remains unchanged.
