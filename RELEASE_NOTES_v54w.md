# My Life Planner v54w — Project Step Pending

Built from the user-confirmed v54v baseline.

## One functional change
Project steps can be marked **Pending** from their three-dot menu. A short reason can be entered or left blank. Pending steps remain visible in project management, but the planner skips them when choosing the project’s Next step. Selecting **Mark active** returns the step to normal Next-step eligibility.

## Protected / unchanged
- Recurring completion and recurring Undo
- Ordinary to-do completion and Undo
- Project-step completion, Undo and reordering
- Waiting For
- iPhone portrait three-dot menu repairs
- Today timed appointment priority
- Backup/export/import and PWA update path
