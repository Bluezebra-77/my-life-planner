# My Life Planner v54x — Ordinary To-do Pending

Built from the user-confirmed v54w baseline.

## One functional change
Ordinary non-recurring to-dos can be marked Pending with an optional reason and returned to Active. Pending is shown in Lists. Completing a Pending to-do clears its Pending state and reason.

## Deliberately unchanged
Project-step Pending, Waiting For, recurring tasks and recurring undo, project-step undo/reordering, ordinary to-do undo, Today timed appointment priority, iPhone portrait menu repairs, backup/restore and PWA update behaviour.

v54x remains a test build until user acceptance.
