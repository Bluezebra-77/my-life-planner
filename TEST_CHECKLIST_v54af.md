# v54af Regression Checklist

## Pending
- Lists → To-dos: incomplete ordinary to-do has visible Pending checkbox and three-dot Mark pending/active.
- Lists → Projects: incomplete project step has visible Pending checkbox and three-dot Mark pending/active.
- Home → Today — Time Sensitive: Pending to-do/project step due today or overdue appears with Pending reason.
- Home → Needs Attention: Pending to-do/project step due today or overdue appears with Pending reason.
- Overdue Pending items show OVERDUE and red treatment.
- Project Next step skips Pending steps; Mark active restores normal Next-step selection.

## Protected workflows
- Today — Time Sensitive still shows ordinary due/overdue items, appointments, recurring tasks, annual dates and cleaning.
- Timed appointments still show time and sort ahead of untimed today items; overdue items remain first.
- Needs Attention still includes its pre-v54ae categories: ordinary actionable to-dos, due cleaning, active project Next steps and Waiting For reviews.
- Recurring task completion advances correctly; Undo last completion still works.
- Timeline direct Delete remains available for dated item types.
- Lists → Projects and Lists → To-dos three-dot menus remain visible on iPhone portrait.
- Project-step Move up/down and Mark incomplete remain functional.
- Ordinary to-do Mark incomplete remains functional.
- Birthday/Anniversary rollover and Quick Add remain functional.
- Home global Timer remains available.
- Backup/export and restore/import remain valid.
- Version shows v54af in Home, About, Developer, version.json and cache metadata.
