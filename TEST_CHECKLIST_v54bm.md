# Development v54bm acceptance checklist

## New Timeline Schedule view
- Timeline opens with All selected and existing Timeline content/period filters behave as before.
- Switching to Schedule does not change saved data.
- Schedule shows Recurring Tasks and Appointments only.
- Each date has a clear day/date heading.
- Within each date, Recurring Tasks appear first.
- A visual gap separates Recurring Tasks from Appointments when both exist on a date.
- Appointments are chronological within the date.
- Ordinary To-dos, To-do steps, Projects, Project steps/deadlines, Cleaning, Annual dates and Pending notes do not appear in Schedule.
- Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, This month and All upcoming filters work in both All and Schedule.
- Tapping Schedule items still opens the existing editor and existing Timeline Delete remains available.
