My Life Planner Help Centre

Short explanations for the main areas of the planner. Your information stays in your browser unless you export or restore a backup.

First time here?

Start with the First Use & Quick Start Guide. It walks through the small amount of setup that makes the planner yours.

Your day

Home is the action dashboard. Check Time Sensitive first, then Today’s Focus, Needs Attention, Projects, Pending and Recurring items. Full collections stay in Lists.

Capture first

Use Brain Inbox when you want to record something quickly and decide where it belongs later. Quick Add is useful when you already know what type of item you are creating.

Daily Rhythm and Evening Routine

Add only the routine steps that are useful to you. They are companions for the day, not tasks that affect Today’s progress.

Today’s Focus

Use Today’s Focus for practical one-off actions you want visible today.

Recurring Tasks

Recurring responsibilities remain due or overdue until completed, then advance to the next occurrence. Undo last completion is available if needed.

Projects and Pending

The first incomplete active project step is shown as Next. Mark a to-do or project step Pending from its three-dot menu when it cannot move forward yet.

Lists and Timeline

Lists are for full management and editing. Timeline is the date-based view for Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 Days, This Month and upcoming work.

Planner Health

Planner Health offers a gentle suggestion when something may need attention. It does not change your records automatically.

Protect your data

Use Settings → Backup and restore to create a manual backup regularly. Automatic daily recovery snapshots are also retained.
