# Development v54bo acceptance checklist

## Update / iPhone
- Existing installed Development app opens normally before update.
- Settings → App maintenance → Check for updates offers v54bo.
- Applying the update performs at most one visible transition/reload and settles on v54bo without flashing/reload loop.
- Closing and reopening the Home Screen app remains stable.

## Brain Inbox
- Add short title only and confirm persistence after reopen.
- Add short title + iPhone photo and confirm persistence after reopen.
- If storage is genuinely insufficient, capture remains open and no phantom item is left behind.

## Timeline
- All preserves the existing Timeline.
- Schedule shows only Recurring Tasks and Appointments.
- Schedule groups by date, recurring first, then appointments chronologically.
- Existing Timeline edit/delete actions still work.

## Protected regression
- Today’s progress Hide/Show works.
- Appointments remain blue with no completion checkbox.
- Lists search, Custom Lists, Pending, Recurring completion/undo, Project Next and backup controls remain available.
