# Development v54bq — iPhone test checklist

1. Upload the complete v54bq package, replacing the Development files.
2. Open the existing installed Development app and use Settings → App maintenance → Check for updates once.
3. Confirm the header shows DEV v54bq and the screen remains stable with no flashing/reload loop.
4. Confirm existing planner information is present.
5. Brain Inbox: add a short title and an iPhone photo; save it.
6. Close and reopen the app; confirm the Brain Inbox entry and photo remain and the photo opens.
7. Convert an attachment item to a To-do or Project and confirm its attachment remains available.
8. Create an Export backup. Do not delete existing data during this test.
9. Timeline: verify All and Schedule; Schedule should group by date, recurring tasks first, then appointments chronologically.
